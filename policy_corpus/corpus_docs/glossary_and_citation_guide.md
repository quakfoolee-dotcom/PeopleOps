# Corpus Glossary and Citation Guide

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Business day | Monday-Friday, excluding a holiday on the employee's assigned company calendar. |
| Registered Home Office | Office and jurisdiction stored in the HR system for employment, payroll, and policy assignment. |
| RFT | Regular Full-Time employee, normally scheduled 30 or more hours per week. |
| RPT | Regular Part-Time employee, normally scheduled 20-29 hours per week. |
| Company-managed device | Device enrolled in Northstar management, security, encryption, patching, and monitoring controls. |
| Confidential information | Non-public employee, customer, contract, source-code, or financial information requiring approved systems and access. |
| Restricted information | Highest-sensitivity information requiring explicit authorization and enhanced controls. |
| Mock action | Educational action that creates no real external or employee-record change. |
