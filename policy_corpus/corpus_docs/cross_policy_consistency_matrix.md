# Cross-Policy Consistency Matrix

| Topic | Authoritative policy | Consistent rule used across the corpus |
|---|---|---|
| Employment categories | POL-BEN-001 | RFT is normally 30+ hours/week; RPT is normally 20-29 hours/week. |
| Vacation/PTO | POL-PTO-001 | PTO is planned discretionary time; holidays are not deducted; medical/protected absences use POL-LEV-001. |
| Holiday calendar | POL-HOL-001 | Calendar follows Registered Home Office, not temporary travel location. |
| Recurring remote work | POL-RWK-001 | Office-Based, Hybrid, or Remote designation; recurring work normally remains within registered jurisdiction. |
| Work outside province/state/country | POL-INT-001 | Any work outside registered jurisdiction requires assessment; international normal limit is 20 business days, with 21-30 exceptional. |
| International security | POL-SEC-001 | Company-managed device, MFA, encryption, VPN/approved network, and explicit approval for Restricted data. |
| Home-office furniture | POL-EQP-001 / POL-EXP-001 | Eligible Hybrid/Remote employees: preapproved reimbursement up to CAD 500 or USD 375 once every 36 months. |
| Internet stipend | POL-EQP-001 | Remote designation: CAD 50 or USD 40 monthly; duplicate expense claim not allowed. |
| Benefits | POL-BEN-001 | RFT generally eligible for core benefits; RPT core coverage normally requires 25+ hours and plan permission. |
| Paid wellness leave | POL-LEV-001 | RFT 80 hours/year; RPT prorated; separate from PTO. |
| Security incident timing | POL-SEC-001 / POL-EQP-001 | Lost/stolen device, credential compromise, or material disclosure reported within one hour. |
| Conduct reporting | POL-CON-001 | Managers escalate serious conduct concerns within one business day, sooner when urgent. |
| HR case safety | POL-HRC-001 | Emergencies use emergency/Security channels; sensitive findings require human review; mock writes require explicit confirmation. |
| Policy precedence | All | Applicable law, formal plan documents, and signed agreements control where they provide different or greater rights. |

## Deliberate multi-document questions supported

1. Can a British Columbia employee work from Germany for six weeks? Use POL-RWK-001, POL-INT-001, POL-SEC-001, and possibly POL-HOL-001/POL-EXP-001.
2. Can employee E-1021 take three PTO days next week? Use structured PTO balance plus POL-PTO-001 and POL-HOL-001.
3. Can a CAD 900 home-office chair be reimbursed? Use POL-EQP-001 and POL-EXP-001, with employee work designation from structured data.
4. Is a part-time employee eligible for medical benefits during leave? Use POL-BEN-001, POL-LEV-001, and structured employment/benefit status.
5. How should a harassment concern be handled? Use POL-CON-001 and POL-HRC-001; do not let the agent make a finding.
