## HOL-1. Purpose

This policy defines company holiday calendars, floating holidays, substitute days, work performed on a holiday, and closures caused by weather, building conditions, public emergencies, or company decisions.

## HOL-2. Assigned holiday calendar

Each employee is assigned a holiday calendar based on the Registered Home Office in the HR system, not the employee's temporary travel location. Vancouver and Toronto employees normally follow the Canada calendar. Seattle and Austin employees normally follow the United States calendar. A written employment agreement or local legal requirement may create a different calendar.

People Operations publishes the next year's calendar by December 1. A calendar may be updated when a government changes an observed date or when Northstar designates an additional closure.

## HOL-3. Eligibility and pay treatment

RFT employees receive holiday pay for the scheduled hours that would normally be worked, up to eight hours, unless a local rule provides more. RPT employees receive holiday pay when the holiday falls on a normally scheduled workday, prorated to the scheduled hours, unless law requires another calculation.

Temporary employees and interns receive holiday pay only when required by law or stated in a written agreement. Contractors are not employees and do not receive company holiday pay.

## HOL-6. Floating holiday

Each RFT and RPT employee receives one floating holiday per calendar year. A floating holiday:

- may be used for a cultural, religious, civic, or personal observance;
- should normally be requested at least five business days in advance;
- is deducted based on the employee's scheduled hours, up to eight hours;
- requires manager approval for operational coverage;
- does not carry over after December 31; and
- is not paid out at separation unless required by law.

A new employee hired on or before September 30 receives the floating holiday for that year. A later hire does not receive it unless the offer letter says otherwise.

## HOL-7. Weekend observance and substitute days

When a fixed-date holiday falls on a weekend, the published calendar identifies the observed weekday. Employees must use the published date rather than independently selecting another day.

An employee required and preapproved to work on a company holiday may receive a substitute paid day, premium pay, or another legally required treatment. The manager must consult People Operations before promising a specific result. A substitute day should normally be used within 60 calendar days.

## HOL-8. Holiday during PTO or leave

A paid company holiday during approved PTO is recorded as a holiday and is not deducted from the employee's PTO balance. Holiday pay during a statutory, medical, personal, or unpaid leave is determined under **POL-LEV-001**, the relevant benefit plan, and applicable law.

## HOL-9. Office closures and delayed openings

Northstar may close a physical office or announce a delayed opening because of severe weather, utility failure, unsafe building conditions, transportation disruption, public emergency, or another material risk.

- Employees able to work safely from an approved location should normally work remotely unless the closure notice states that operations are suspended.
- Employees who cannot work remotely must contact their manager. People Operations will determine pay or time-recording treatment based on employment status, local requirements, and the closure notice.
- Employees must not travel to a closed office to retrieve equipment without Facilities or Security approval.
- A school or dependent-care closure does not automatically close Northstar. Employees should discuss PTO, flexible hours, or leave options with the manager.

## HOL-10. Essential coverage

Some support, security, infrastructure, and customer operations may require holiday coverage. Managers must identify essential coverage in advance, use an equitable rotation where practical, and confirm time-recording or substitute-day treatment before the shift.

## HOL-11. Religious and cultural accommodation

An employee who needs time away for an observance not on the assigned calendar may use the floating holiday, PTO, a schedule adjustment, unpaid time, or an accommodation. Requests are handled individually and must not be denied for a discriminatory or retaliatory reason.

## HOL-12. Responsibilities and records

People Operations owns the calendars. Managers communicate coverage and approve floating holidays. Employees verify their assigned calendar and promptly report an incorrect assignment. Holiday calendars, closure notices, substitute-day approvals, and pay adjustments are retained in the HR or payroll system.

## HOL-13. Examples

- A Vancouver employee temporarily working in Germany continues to follow the Canada holiday calendar unless an approved international-work decision says otherwise.
- A Toronto employee on PTO from December 24 through December 31, 2026 is not charged PTO for December 25 or December 28.
- A Seattle employee required to work on November 27 must have the arrangement and resulting pay or substitute day confirmed in advance.
- A remote-capable employee normally works from home when the assigned office closes because of snow, unless the notice suspends operations.
