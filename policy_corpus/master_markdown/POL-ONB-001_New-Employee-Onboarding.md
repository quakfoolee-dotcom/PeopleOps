## ONB-1. Purpose

This policy establishes a consistent, secure, and welcoming onboarding process from accepted offer through the first 90 calendar days. It defines required records, access, equipment, training, role clarity, and ownership.

## ONB-2. Principles

Onboarding must:

- collect only necessary information and protect it appropriately;
- provide access based on least privilege and verified role need;
- make policy, conduct, security, and reporting expectations clear;
- provide the equipment and support required for the approved work designation;
- assign accountable owners and due dates; and
- avoid granting access before required approvals, identity checks, or agreements are complete.

## ONB-3. Roles

- **People Operations:** Owns the onboarding case, employment records, policy notices, eligibility, and completion tracking.
- **Hiring manager:** Defines role outcomes, schedule, work designation, access needs, training, and team integration.
- **IT:** Prepares devices, accounts, standard software, and technical support.
- **Information Security:** Applies identity, access, device, and data controls and assigns security training.
- **Finance/Payroll:** Establishes payroll, tax, expense, and purchasing records.
- **Employee:** Completes accurate forms and training, protects credentials, and raises missing or inaccessible items promptly.

## ONB-4. Preboarding - accepted offer through day before start

The following should be completed or scheduled before the start date:

1. signed offer and required agreements;
2. identity and right-to-work process, where applicable;
3. tax, payroll, emergency contact, and banking information through approved secure systems;
4. employment category, Registered Home Office, manager, cost centre, and work designation;
5. benefits eligibility notice date;
6. background or credential checks authorized for the role;
7. equipment and shipping request under **POL-EQP-001**;
8. account and role-based access requests;
9. first-week schedule, orientation invitations, and onboarding buddy; and
10. accommodation or accessibility needs handled confidentially by People Operations.

Sensitive information must not be collected through ordinary email when a secure form is available.

## ONB-5. Day one requirements

On day one, the employee should receive:

- identity and account activation support;
- manager welcome and role overview;
- approved work schedule and work-location expectations;
- access to policies, reporting channels, and the employee portal;
- equipment inventory and acceptable-use acknowledgement;
- payroll and timekeeping instructions; and
- a first-week plan.

A manager must not ask a new employee to share credentials or use another employee's account while access is pending.

## ONB-6. Required training deadlines

| Training or acknowledgement | Normal deadline |
|---|---:|
| Account security, phishing, MFA, and incident reporting | Within 3 business days |
| Workplace conduct, harassment prevention, and reporting | Within 10 business days |
| Privacy and data-classification training | Within 10 business days |
| Role-specific safety, compliance, or customer training | Before performing the controlled activity and no later than 30 days |
| Manager responsibilities, if applicable | Within 30 calendar days |

Access to sensitive systems may be withheld or suspended until required training is complete.

## ONB-7. First-week integration

The manager should explain team goals, decision rights, communication norms, core collaboration hours, stakeholders, current priorities, and how performance will be assessed. The employee should meet key team members and know how to request help.

For Remote or Hybrid employees, the manager should verify workspace readiness, meeting access, time-zone expectations, and planned onsite requirements. Recurring work-location rules are governed by **POL-RWK-001**.

## ONB-8. 30-, 60-, and 90-day checkpoints

### 30-day checkpoint

Confirm role clarity, essential access, required training, payroll accuracy, benefit enrollment status, equipment, and immediate support needs.

### 60-day checkpoint

Review performance feedback, collaboration, workload, learning plan, and suitability of any recurring remote arrangement that was not established in the offer.

### 90-day checkpoint

Assess progress against initial outcomes, complete outstanding onboarding items, confirm ongoing development priorities, and close or extend the onboarding case. A checkpoint is not a guarantee of employment or a substitute for ordinary performance management.

## ONB-9. Benefits and payroll

People Operations issues eligibility and enrollment notices under **POL-BEN-001**. Employees must complete elections within the stated window. Payroll questions should be reported promptly and not deferred until the 30-day checkpoint.

## ONB-10. Equipment and home-office setup

IT provides the standard kit for the employee's designation under **POL-EQP-001**. Employees should not purchase a laptop or other technology expecting reimbursement. Eligible ergonomic furniture requires preapproval and is subject to the policy cap.

Equipment shipped before the start date must remain unopened by anyone other than the employee and must be stored securely. Loss or damage must be reported immediately.

## ONB-11. Access control and segregation of duties

Access must be requested by an authorized manager, approved by the system owner where required, and limited to the role. Privileged, financial, HR, production, and Restricted-data access requires enhanced review. Access should be tested, but not broadened merely for convenience.

Changes identified during onboarding must use the same approval process. Shared accounts are prohibited unless Security has approved a technical exception.

## ONB-12. Incomplete, delayed, or failed onboarding items

A missing item should be assigned to an owner and tracked in the onboarding case. The employee should not be penalized for a company-caused delay. However, Northstar may postpone controlled work, restrict access, or adjust duties when identity, authorization, training, equipment, or security requirements are incomplete.

A material issue, such as inability to verify work authorization, suspected record falsification, safety risk, or harassment concern, must be escalated under **POL-HRC-001**.

## ONB-13. Records and privacy

Onboarding records are retained in approved HR, payroll, learning, asset, and access systems. Copies should not be stored in manager personal drives. Medical or accommodation information is stored separately with restricted access.

## ONB-14. Examples

- A new Remote employee receives a company laptop and approved accessories; the employee does not buy a personal laptop for reimbursement.
- A finance employee may start general orientation while privileged payment access remains pending required approval.
- An employee hired as RFT on September 10 receives the benefits eligibility timeline described in **POL-BEN-001** and must enroll within the notice window.
- A missing security-training completion may lead to temporary access restriction but should be tracked and resolved, not ignored.
